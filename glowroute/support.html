HELP WITH GLOWROUTE

Support

Fast answers for gameplay, accessibility, local data, and Glowroute Club.

A route fails even though it reaches the flag

Glowroute stops at the first rule violation. Check the highlighted cell and message: you may be carrying the wrong spark, crossing a closed gate, touching a breaker, or leaving a spark undelivered.

How do I restore Glowroute Club?

Open Settings in Glowroute and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure the device uses the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I cancel or change a subscription?

Apple manages billing. Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Glowroute cannot cancel a subscription on your behalf.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The training campaign and scheduled Daily and Weekly puzzles are generated on-device. StoreKit may need a connection to start, restore, or refresh a subscription.

What is the Daily Archive?

Glowroute Club can replay the previous seven Daily Sets. Archive practice does not repair or extend the live Daily streak.

How do I disable sound, haptics, or motion?

Open Glowroute Settings to disable sound or haptics. Glowroute also follows the iPhone Reduce Motion accessibility setting.

How do I share diagnostics?

Open Glowroute Settings and choose Share Diagnostics. Nothing is sent until you pick a destination in the iOS share sheet.
